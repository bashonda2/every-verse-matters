You are not merely saved as an individual; you are being prepared to participate in God's eternal kingdom, with all the weight and glory that entails. The fruitfulness promised to Abraham—biological, spiritual, intellectual, and authoritative—becomes, through covenant, the fruitfulness you are invited to embody. In your family, in your priesthood service, in your sphere of influence, you are living out the promise that Abraham's descendants shall be fruitful and that from them shall come those who will govern and bless others in God's name."
