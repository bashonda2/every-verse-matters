The specific mention of 'bearing a burden' at the gates — the normal commerce and work of ordinary life — invites us to consider whether we are truly setting aside the Sabbath or simply maintaining our normal rhythm with a few religious activities added. The prophecy's warning is not meant to induce guilt but to wake us to reality: the Sabbath is not a quaint tradition but a covenant boundary.
