This might manifest as the departure of destructive habits, the arrival of spiritual clarity, the healing of relationships, or the opening of doors that were previously closed. The principle of verse 51 is that obedience and blessing are not separated; they are 'selfsame day' experiences. Practically, this means examining the covenants we have made (and those we are preparing to make) with fresh eyes: Am I keeping them with exactness, as Israel kept Passover? Am I trusting that God's deliverance comes not through my scheming but through His hand? Am I living as one who has been 'brought out' of the bondage of sin and death, organized and moving forward under divine leadership toward the promised land? The final verse of the Passover chapter calls us to move from promise to fulfillment, from obedience to deliverance, in the confidence that God's word is true and His arm is mighty to save."
