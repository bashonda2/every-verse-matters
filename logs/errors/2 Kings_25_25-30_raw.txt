(4) The covenant is resilient, capable of surviving the loss of its external markers (the Temple, the kingdom, the land) and enduring in its most reduced form (daily bread from a foreign king's table). Modern covenant people who face loss, exile from the life they planned, or circumstances that strip away former glory may find in Jehoiachin a type of faithful endurance. The message is not 'you will be restored immediately to what you lost' but rather 'you will be sustained, day by day, as long as you live.'
