      "application": "This verse is deeply sobering. It establishes that judgment is not arbitrary or excessive but proportional, fair, and based on human choice and responsibility. The apostate cannot claim ignorance ('when I called'), cannot claim incomprehension ('when I spake'), and cannot claim innocence ('ye did evil before mine eyes'). But it also implies an urgent positive application: God *is* calling. God *is* speaking. The question each reader must face is: Will I answer when He calls? Will I truly hear and obey when He speaks? Will I choose what delights Him, or what delights me? For Latter-day Saints, the calling comes through living prophets, through personal revelation in prayer, through the witness of the Spirit. The verse demands not vague spirituality but specific, deliberate choices. It asks: When the prophet speaks, do I answer or resist? When the Spirit whispers, do I listen or rationalize? When covenant requires sacrifice, do I choose what God delights in or what I find comfortable? Verse 12 is not meant to induce despair but to clarify stakes and summon us to radical fidelity."
