This calls us to humility: our dominion is not a license to exploit unknowingly. It calls us also to responsibility: we must grow in understanding of creation—through science, observation, and revelation—so that we can steward wisely. For those who work in marine science, ornithology, or environmental conservation, Psalm 8:9 affirms the spiritual significance of your work. You are not merely studying interesting phenomena; you are learning to better understand and care for the creatures and pathways encompassed within human dominion. For all members, the verse suggests a practical application: environmental stewardship is covenant stewardship. How we treat the birds of the air and the fish of the sea reflects our fidelity to the dominion mandate. Pollution, reckless overfishing, habitat destruction, and species extinction represent a breach of the stewardship entrusted in Psalm 8:9.
