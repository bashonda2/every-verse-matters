Both brothers know, without being commanded, that they should bring offerings to God. The impulse to worship—to acknowledge God through gift-giving—is woven into human nature in covenant. Yet the verse also introduces a principle that will be developed throughout Scripture: what we bring to God matters, and why we bring it matters even more. In modern covenant life, this teaches that our offerings to God—our time, our service, our substance—should be given with intention, with the best we have, not with the remainder.
