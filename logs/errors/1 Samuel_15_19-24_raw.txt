True confession requires accepting responsibility for our choices without excuse. Saul's confession fails because he is still focused on justifying himself—'I feared the people' is an explanation, not an acceptance of accountability. Real repentance requires us to say: I chose this. I am responsible. There is no excuse. I am changing my posture and orientation. The test of genuine repentance is whether we stop the behavior and change the underlying priorities that led to it. Saul continues in verse 25 to ask for validation before the people—he has not truly changed his fundamental concern (what people think) that led to his original sin. Have we?
