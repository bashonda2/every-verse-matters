For adult children suffering from the consequences of parental apostasy, the verse suggests that while they may inherit difficult circumstances, God's grace is not entirely withdrawn—they are not beyond redemption if they choose covenant fidelity for themselves.\n\nFor Latter-day Saints, the application runs deeper through temple work: the sealing of families and proxy ordinances assume that righteous ancestors' work can benefit descendants, and conversely, that descendants' righteousness and faithfulness can honor and extend blessing to ancestors.
