This raises the question: If legitimate leaders refuse the role (verse 7), do illegitimate ones fill the void? The implication is that communities *require* guidance, for good or ill. For members of the Church, this suggests that refusing to serve in callings — accepting the call to teach, lead, guide within family and community — leaves a vacuum that may be filled by misdirection. The answer to verse 12's crisis is not despair but active commitment to true leadership — leadership characterized by knowledge of the covenant way, by willingness to sacrifice for the flock, and by genuine love for those being guided. In the final analysis, verse 12 diagnoses a crisis of spiritual disorientation and calls for renewal of legitimate, faithful, loving guidance."
